# 🇭🇹 Haiti Culture Connection

A beautiful Streamlit website celebrating the rich culture, history, and people of Haiti.

## Features

- 🇭🇹 Rich Haitian-themed design with blue, red, and gold colors
- 📜 History of Haiti – the first independent Black republic
- 🎵 Music – Compas, Rara, Meringue, and Vodou rhythms
- 🎨 Art – Naïve art, sculpture, and Vodou flags
- 🍲 Cuisine – Griot, Soup Joumou, Poulet Creole, and more
- 🗣️ Language – French and Haitian Creole
- 🎉 Festivals – Carnival, Rara, Christmas, and Independence Day
- 📤 Logo upload feature
- 📱 Fully responsive – works on mobile and desktop
